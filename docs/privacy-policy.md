# PropheSee Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

## What stays on your device

Your name, training sessions, scores, streaks and trainer request history are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app or choosing Reset progress removes them.

## What the PropheSee server receives

To run fair blind trials, the server chooses and seals each target and receives your answer for that round so it can open the seal. Each install gets an anonymous account id; we do not ask for your email or phone number to train. Trial data is kept in memory and deleted within 6 hours.

## Trainer session requests

If you request a 1-to-1 trainer session, the name, contact details, preferred time and notes you enter are sent to our trainers so they can contact you. They are used only to arrange and deliver that session.

## Payments

Memberships are sold through the App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. We never see your card details. RevenueCat receives your anonymous account id and purchase history so your membership works on your devices.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use third-party tracking or analytics.

## Children

PropheSee is not directed at children under 13.
